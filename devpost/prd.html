Features and acceptance criteria

Chat (customer ↔ seller)
  • A customer message appears on both phones within about 2 seconds.
  • Offered options show as buttons; tapping one sends it as the reply.
  • Payment links show as a link card that opens the pay page.
  • Only the seller sends seller messages. Nothing is sent automatically.
Read order
  • Fills items (product, size or variant, quantity, unit price) from the chat since the last finished order. Several items supported.
  • Found (with quote), Missing ("Never mentioned") or Set by you.
  • The AI never invents a price. Seller values are never overwritten.
  • On failure: "Couldn't read this conversation. Try again." The seller can type the details instead.
  • "Read again" appears after new customer messages.
Settling what's sold
  • The seller types any item value or the delivery fee.
  • Ask customer: up to 3 options (not for price).
  • Total = quantity × unit price + delivery fee, in US dollars, always visible.
Drafted message
  • One draft at a time, labelled Draft, editable.
  • Asks only for what's missing. After Request payment it holds the order number, total and link. Thank-you and refund messages after Paid and Refunded.
  • Pressing Send twice sends once.
Seller-approved agent
  • After each customer message, at most one proposal: Ask, Request payment, Remind, Refund.
  • Each has a one-line reason quoting the customer.
  • Approve runs it; Dismiss hides it until the next customer message.
  • It can't move money or send without the seller's tap. Amounts always come from the order card.
  • If the AI is down, the order card buttons still work.
Request payment
  • Only when nothing is missing and the total is above $0.
  • Freezes items and total and creates a one-time pay link. The PayPal order carries items, delivery as shipping, USD total and the order number.
  • Pressing twice makes one link; paying never charges twice.
  • Items can't change afterwards: cancel and start a new order instead.
Pay page
  • Shows the order exactly as on the seller's card.
  • PayPal buttons (sandbox). Name and shipping address from PayPal.
  • The server captures and checks the amount before marking Paid.
  • Paid orders show "already paid"; cancelled orders show "cancelled".
  • Closing PayPal's window or a decline lets the customer try again.
Paid, refund, cancel, orders, reset
  • Paid within about 2 seconds, with payer name, email, shipping address and transaction ID.
  • Refund: Paid orders only, full amount, confirmation "This can't be undone", once only, shows refund ID; PayPal's refusal reason is shown if it refuses.
  • Cancel before payment; the pay page then refuses payment.
  • Orders tab: number, customer, items, total, status, payer, date.
  • Reset demo restarts at #1001. Data survives a refresh.

States and boundaries
  • Empty inbox: "No messages yet. Type as the customer on the left."
  • AI busy, AI limit reached, PayPal unreachable: clear messages, nothing changes on the order.
  • Seller screen closed during payment: shows Paid when reopened.
  • Free hosting asleep: first open may take up to a minute.
Deferred and non-goals
  • Deferred: real WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger; live reading; unclear details and item editing; partial refunds, disputes, chargebacks; payments outside the app; login and settings.
  • Non-goals: nothing sent or charged without the seller's tap; no catalog; no bare links or bank details; no platform branding; no analytics.
Open questions
  • Refund window is 180 days per PayPal's docs; whether PayPal returns its fee on refunds isn't confirmed (doesn't affect the sandbox demo).
  • Exact payer fields in the capture response: verify with the first sandbox call.
