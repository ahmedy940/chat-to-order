chat-to-order: Chat to Paid

Keep selling in your chat. The conversation becomes the order, the order becomes a PayPal payment, and the payment, the address and any refund stay attached to that order.

Target: PayPal AI Hackathon · Thu 12 Nov 2026, 12:00 pm Pacific · Canonical file: devpost/scope.md

The unique kernel, in 4 moves

AI

1 · Chat → order

The AI reads the chat, fills the order card, marks what's missing, drafts one question. The seller sends it.

Seller

2 · Order → pay page

Request payment freezes the order and drafts a link to a page that shows exactly what was agreed.

PayPal

3 · Customer pays

Items, delivery and total, with PayPal's buttons. Name and address come from their PayPal account.

Seller

4 · Paid → refund

The order turns Paid by itself. A refund is one approved tap on the same order.

The agent watches the chat and proposes the next step (ask, request payment, remind, refund). The seller approves each one. It never moves money or messages a customer on its own.

What "working" looks like: the judge's demo

One hosted link, two phones side by side. Step through it.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the order turns Paid on its own, already carrying the customer's address, and the refund needs one tap with no searching in PayPal.

Who it's for

A one-person or tiny-team seller (for example, clothing) who sells through chat in English, takes payment with PayPal, and has no full online store.

Today: orders in a notebook, amounts typed into payment links, scrolling chats for an address, checking PayPal to see who paid, finding the right payment in PayPal to refund it.

Boundary

In this build

  • Demo page: customer phone + seller phone
  • Read order button: items, size, quantity, price, delivery fee; Found / Missing / Set by you
  • Seller settles what's sold, or offers up to 3 options
  • One drafted message at a time; only the seller sends
  • Request payment → pay-for-this-order page → PayPal sandbox → Paid with payer name and address
  • Full refund after the seller approves; cancel before payment
  • Seller-approved agent: Ask, Request payment, Remind, Refund
  • Orders tab, Reset demo, hosted URL

Later

  • Automatic, live reading
  • Unclear and clashing details, editing items

  • Payments outside the app
  • Product list, customers view, analytics
  • Login, several sellers with their own PayPal
